Workday is unforgiving about structure. A beautiful two-column PDF can look empty to the parser.

## Failure patterns we see constantly

- **Two-column layouts** — experience lands in the wrong field or disappears
- **Headers / footers** — contact info never maps to candidate profile
- **Tables for everything** — skills and dates scramble
- **Icons replacing labels** — "📧" is not an email field
- **Image-based resumes** — zero extractable text

## The cleanup checklist

1. Single column, top to bottom
2. Standard section headings: Experience, Education, Skills
3. Plain text contact line under your name
4. Dates in a consistent format next to each role
5. Export PDF from Word/Google Docs — not a design tool screenshot

## After format: vocabulary

A clean parse with weak keyword overlap still ranks low. Match the requisition language in bullets that reflect work you actually did.
