Tailoring is not rewriting your career for every posting. It's aligning vocabulary and emphasis so the ATS and the recruiter recognize the same person the JD asked for.

Skip this and you get [silent rejection](/blog/job-application-rejection-reasons-ats-vs-human). Overdo it and you get a keyword smoothie nobody believes.

## The 20-minute tailor

### 1. Extract the JD spine (5 min)

Highlight only:

- Hard skills and tools
- Domain nouns (the work, not the vibes)
- Required years / level signals
- Must-have certifications

Ignore "passionate," "self-starter," and culture adjectives unless they appear as hard requirements.

### 2. Map to proof you already have (5 min)

For each must-have, write one line: *where did I do this?* If you can't point to a role or project, don't add the keyword. Fabrication fails interviews harder than ATS filters.

### 3. Rewrite 4–6 bullets (8 min)

Take your most relevant role and rebuild the top bullets to lead with JD language + outcome:

**Before:** Helped the sales team improve processes.  
**After:** Built Salesforce dashboards that cut outbound SDR reporting time from weekly to daily.

Same job. Words the posting recognizes.

### 4. Sync the skills section (2 min)

Mirror the JD's tool names. Keep the list honest and scannable — not a graveyard. Details in [ATS keyword matching](/blog/ats-keyword-matching-complete-guide-2026).

## What not to tailor

- Employment dates and employer names
- Titles you never held
- Metrics you can't explain on a call
- A brand-new career narrative every Tuesday

Change emphasis. Don't invent a parallel life.

## Format still matters

A perfectly tailored two-column Canva PDF can still parse empty. Keep the [single-column, parse-safe rules](/blog/resume-parse-errors-ats-fix-checklist) while you edit language — especially on [Workday](/blog/why-resume-rejected-by-workday) and [Greenhouse](/blog/greenhouse-ats-resume-tips-2026).

## Measure once, then send

Run the tailored file against that JD with a [free ATS resume scanner](/blog/free-ats-resume-scanner-20260719) / [HireReady match check](https://hire-ready.app/#analyzer). If the gap list is short and honest, apply. If it's long, fix bullets — don't spam the skills footer.
