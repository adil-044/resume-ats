# PRODUCT.md — HireReady

## Register
**brand** (marketing landing is primary) with a **product** app shell (dashboard/workspace).

## Platform
`web`

## Users
Job seekers filtered by ATS (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever). Frustrated, time-poor, want a clear match score and a fix — not another keyword checker.

## Purpose
Analyze a resume against a job description, surface gaps, rewrite for ATS compatibility, export. Core features permanently free.

## Positioning
Confrontational clarity: “Stop getting ghosted by ATS.” Not soft career coaching. Not Jobscan-with-extra-steps — we rewrite, not just score.

## Brand personality
Editorial restraint. Graphite ink. Copper signal. Warm paper as *artifact* (the resume), not as page background. Confident, terse, precise.

## Anti-references
- Violet/cyan neon SaaS glow
- Glassmorphism card grids
- Ping-dot pill eyebrows on every section
- Sketchy/hand-drawn SVG illustrations
- Cream/sand full-page backgrounds
- “AI metric hero” big-number templates

## Accessibility
Body text ≥4.5:1 on graphite. Copper CTAs with dark ink text. Reduced-motion for all GSAP/R3F.

## Conversion & proof
Primary CTA → auth / analyzer. Proof via teaser scan + testimonials. Trust: free core, encrypted, no card.
